# Design Specification Rules (Always Active)

All UI/UX development and design updates in this project must strictly comply with `DESIGN.md`.

## Strict Prohibitions
1. **NO Blue or Purple Gradients**:
   - Strictly ban any linear, radial, or conic gradients containing blue, purple, indigo, cyan, violet, or magenta hues.
   - Strictly ban ambient glowing halos, purple/blue blurred background hazes, or neon bloom effects.
2. **Minimalist Flat Geometric Style**:
   - Use flat, solid color fills with high geometric contrast.
   - Minimalist crisp borders (`1px solid`), sharp or micro corner radii (`0px ~ 4px`), pure circular geometry for wheels/discs.
   - No excessive drop-shadow blurs or skeuomorphic textures.
   - Snappy, mechanical, crisp interactions.
